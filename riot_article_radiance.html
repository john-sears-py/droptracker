Radiance Merch Store Update
Here’s how to get Radiance product from the Riot Merch Store.

Are you ready for Radiance? Riftbound’s newest set is coming soon. Here are all the details on how to purchase product from the Riot Merch Store.

Players in North America and Europe can sign up to be selected to purchase one Radiance Booster Display from the Riot Games Merch Store. Selected fans will get an email with a purchase link, with a 24-hour time limit for completing checkout.

Drawing Dates

  • Email Sign-Up Window: September 25-30, 9:00 AM PT
  • Drawing Selection Begins: October 5, approximately 9:00 AM PT
  • Fulfillment of Orders Begins: October 23
